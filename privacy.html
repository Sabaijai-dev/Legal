Privacy Policy

Trading Academy · Last updated: 6 November 2026

EnglishРусскийTerms of Use

This policy explains what data the Trading Academy mobile app (“the app”, package com.sabaijai.trading) processes and why. The app is published by Sabaijai (“we”).

In short

  • No registration and no account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps. We never sell data.
  • Your lesson progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We only store a notification token and the tickers you follow — and only if you turn on pattern alerts.

Data we process

DataWhyWhere / whoHow long
Push notification token, the tickers and categories you follow, app language To send pattern alerts you turned on (Pro plan) Our database at Supabase (Sydney, Australia); delivered via Expo Push and Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service Until you turn alerts off, your Pro plan ends, or the app is uninstalled (the token stops working and is deleted)

We never see your payment card details: payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database and file hosting
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — push delivery
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — app distribution and payments
  • Binance — public price data for demo trading (no account, no personal data sent by us)

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The notification database can only be read by our server; the app can only add or remove its own token.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn alerts off in the app (or remove all followed tickers) — your token and preferences are deleted from our database.
  • Reset progress in Profile, or uninstall the app, to delete data stored on the device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play or App Store settings.
  • You may ask us to access or delete any data related to you by email (see below). Because there are no accounts, please include your notification token or purchase receipt if you have it.

Age

The app is intended for adults (18+). It is educational and does not provide investment advice.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the app.

  • Вы можете запросить доступ к данным или их удаление по email (ниже). Аккаунтов нет, поэтому приложите, если есть, токен уведомлений или чек покупки.
