Privacy Policy

Chartcompass · Last updated: 10 November 2026

EnglishРусскийTerms of Use

This policy explains what data the Chartcompass mobile app (“the app”, package com.sabaijai.trading) processes and why. The app is published by Sabaijai (“we”).

In short

  • No registration and no account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads inside the app. We never sell data.
  • Your lesson progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics (which screens and lessons are opened, whether the subscription screen was shown and what happened with a purchase) to improve the app. For this we use our own database and Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase also uses your device’s advertising ID to tell us which of our ad campaigns (Google Ads) brought installs and trials. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • On our server we also keep your anonymous answers to the welcome screens and, if you turn on pattern alerts, a notification token and the tickers you follow.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database and file hosting
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Google Firebase — Firebase Cloud Messaging (push delivery) and Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics); Apple Push Notification service — push delivery on iOS
  • Google Ads — measuring our ad campaigns (no ads are shown inside the app)
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — app distribution and payments
  • Binance — public price data for demo trading (no account, no personal data sent by us)

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The notification database can only be read by our server; the app can only add or remove its own token.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn alerts off in the app (or remove all followed tickers) — your token and preferences are deleted from our database.
  • Reset progress in Profile, or uninstall the app, to delete data stored on the device.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play or App Store settings.
  • You may ask us to access or delete any data related to you by email (see below). Because there are no accounts, please include your notification token or purchase receipt if you have it.

Age

The app is intended for adults (18+). It is educational and does not provide investment advice.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Безопасность

Все данные передаются по зашифрованному соединению (HTTPS). Базу уведомлений может читать только наш сервер; приложение может только добавить или удалить свой токен.

  • Рекламный идентификатор можно сбросить или удалить, а персонализацию рекламы — отключить в настройках телефона (Android: Настройки → Google → Реклама).
  • Подпиской можно управлять и отменить её в настройках Google Play или App Store.
  • Вы можете запросить доступ к данным или их удаление по email (ниже). Аккаунтов нет, поэтому приложите, если есть, токен уведомлений или чек покупки.
